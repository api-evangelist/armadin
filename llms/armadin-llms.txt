# Armadin

> Armadin is an AI-native cybersecurity company building the "Ultimate Attacker" — an autonomous, agentic offensive security platform that continuously identifies, proves, and prioritizes exploitable risk across enterprise attack surfaces. Founded in 2025 and launched publicly in March 2026, Armadin is headquartered in San Francisco, CA, with approximately 60 employees.

Armadin's core thesis: as AI-powered "Hyperattacks" — sophisticated, machine-speed, multi-modal cyberattacks — become the new norm, traditional human-led defenses are no longer sufficient. Armadin answers this with an intelligent fleet of AI agents and elite human red teamers who find all attack paths, all the time.

The company has raised $189.9M in combined Seed and Series A funding (the largest such combined round in cybersecurity history), led by Accel, with participation from Google Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, Menlo Ventures, 8VC, Ballistic Ventures, and In-Q-Tel (the CIA's venture arm).

## Platform Capabilities

Armadin's platform is described as the first of its kind: a unified, scalable, and ongoing red team capability that finds all exploitable risks continuously. Key capabilities include:

- **Agentic attacker swarms**: AI agents that reason, plan, and adapt like real adversaries — not scripted scanners.
- **Continuous coverage**: Nation-state-level offensive capability operating inside customer networks 24/7.
- **Multi-modal attack surface coverage**: Operates across the entire external and internal attack surface.
- **Validated kill chains**: Every finding comes with a proven, end-to-end attack path — not just a list of CVEs.
- **Prioritized remediation**: Guidance ranked by business impact, helping teams close the most dangerous gaps first.
- **Human-in-the-loop safety**: Expert human oversight and built-in controls ensure safety and privacy throughout.
- **AI model flexibility**: Designed to assess using multiple leading frontier models, not locked to a single one.

## Key Concepts & Terminology

- **Hyperattacks**: Armadin's term for AI-powered, machine-speed, multi-modal cyberattacks that far outpace traditional human-led defenses.
- **The Ultimate Attacker**: Armadin's platform concept — an AI system trained to the standards of world-class red teams that continuously finds exploitable risk before real adversaries do.
- **Agentic attacker swarm**: Armadin's fleet of AI agents that work in coordination to explore, adapt, and attack enterprise environments like a real adversary would.
- **Exploitable risk**: Armadin distinguishes between theoretical vulnerabilities and proven, exploitable risks — ones with a validated end-to-end kill chain.
- **Armadin Red**: Armadin's most advanced assessment offering — a comprehensive evaluation of organizational readiness for the AI threat era.
- **The Hyperattack Gap**: The widening security gap between the speed of AI-driven offense and the pace of human-led defense.
